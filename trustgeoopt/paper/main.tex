\subsection{Sprint 2 evidence validation}
Independent six-rate CPU tuning with two development seeds, followed by five
fresh evaluation seeds and 1,200 steps per trial, removes the initial stationary
quadratic AdamW deficit. A stochastic late-loss gap remains. The projected online
counterexample of Reddi et al.\ \cite{amsgrad2018} is reproduced separately under
its specific assumptions. These findings are synthetic evidence, not neural
benchmark results or a new convergence theorem.
Whole-seed resampling and held-out configurations show that alignment has only
a small, regime-dependent conditional advantage at short leads; history does
not show robust unseen incremental value. Exact quadratic geometry explains
same-step harmful labels with oracle information. Fixed step clipping, momentum
reset and conservative rate safeguards do not robustly close the observed noisy
gap. Full geometric optimizer implementation remains deferred. Detailed source,
configuration and statistical provenance are in the research Sprint 2 reports.
